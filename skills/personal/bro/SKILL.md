# Bro

Say your last reply again the way you would say it out loud to a smart friend who is not in the weeds.

- Same facts, same decision, same next step. Add nothing new.
- Lead with the one thing they need to know or do.
- No jargon, acronyms, or file paths unless they need them to act. If a term is unavoidable, say what it means in the same sentence.
- Two to five short sentences. No headings, bullets, or code.
- Follow `~/.agents/STYLE.md`.
